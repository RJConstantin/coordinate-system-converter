01   Add coordinate data

Paste a table or upload a CSV.

Copy directly from Excel, including the header row. Extra columns such as Point, Name, Legal Location, or Notes are preserved in the output.

Tab, comma, or semicolon separated tables are accepted.
No coordinate table loaded yet.
02   Coordinate systems

Choose the source and destination.

The coordinate system names and EPSG codes are shown together so they match what you see in ArcGIS Pro.

Conversions between projected systems on the same datum use the listed projection parameters. Cross-datum conversions are handled as coordinate-system conversions without a high-accuracy datum transformation grid.
03   Coordinate columns

Tell the converter which columns contain the coordinates.

For projected systems, X is Easting and Y is Northing. For geographic systems, X is Longitude and Y is Latitude.

04   Convert coordinates

Add a coordinate table to continue.

Your original columns will be preserved. Converted coordinate columns are added to a new downloadable CSV.
